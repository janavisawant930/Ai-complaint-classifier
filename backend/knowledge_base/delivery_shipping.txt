# Delivery and Shipping Policies

## 1. Delayed Deliveries and Tracking Status
- **Policy**: Deliveries exceeding the estimated delivery date (EDD) are prioritized with logistics partner.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Trigger immediate courier status ping to fetch real-time GPS location.
  2. If delivery is delayed beyond 3 business days of EDD, offer shipping fee refund and store credit voucher.
- **Classification Category**: Delivery
- **Priority**: Medium
- **Typical Confidence**: 90-95%

## 2. Lost Packages and Non-Delivery
- **Policy**: If courier tracking shows no scan updates for 5 consecutive days or parcel is marked delivered but not received.
- **Resolution**: Open lost shipment investigation with carrier. Dispatch free replacement order within 24 hours or issue 100% full refund if replacement is out of stock.
- **Classification Category**: Delivery
- **Priority**: Medium

## 3. Delivery to Wrong Address or Courier Issues
- **Policy**: Packages mistakenly delivered to neighbor or wrong geographical address.
- **Resolution**: Re-route courier agent for retrieval or issue replacement to customer's verified shipping address.
- **Classification Category**: Delivery
- **Priority**: Medium
