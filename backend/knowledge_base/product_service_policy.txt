# Product and Service Quality Policies

## 1. Damaged, Broken, or Defective Products Upon Delivery
- **Policy**: If an item arrives physically damaged, cracked, broken, or malfunctioning out of the box, the customer is entitled to an immediate 100% free replacement or full refund if reported within 7 days of delivery.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Validate complaint and request photo/video evidence of damaged item and outer packaging.
  2. Initiate return pickup or waive return depending on damage severity.
  3. Dispatch expedited replacement order at zero extra charge within 24 hours, or process a full refund to original payment method.
- **Classification Category**: Product/Service
- **Priority**: High
- **Typical Confidence**: 94-98%

## 2. Poor Quality, Defective Functionality, and Warranty Claims
- **Policy**: Products covered under manufacturer or standard 1-year warranty can be repaired or replaced free of charge for hardware defects.
- **Resolution**: Arrange service technician visit or provide prepaid shipping label for warranty repair/replacement center.
- **Classification Category**: Product/Service
- **Priority**: High

## 3. Wrong Item Delivered or Missing Parts / Accessories
- **Policy**: If the customer receives an item different from ordered or package is missing components/cables/accessories.
- **Resolution**: Dispatch correct item/missing accessories via priority courier within 24 hours with complimentary return pickup for incorrect items.
- **Classification Category**: Product/Service
- **Priority**: High
