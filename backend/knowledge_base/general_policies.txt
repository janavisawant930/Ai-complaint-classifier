# General Inquiries, Feedback, and Other Policies

## 1. General Customer Feedback and Staff Behavior
- **Policy**: Customer opinions, compliments, service suggestions, store timing questions, or feedback regarding agent conduct.
- **Resolution**: Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours, route constructive feedback to departmental leads, and log ticket in CRM.
- **Classification Category**: Other
- **Priority**: Low
- **Typical Confidence**: 85-90%

## 2. Policy Clarifications and Miscellaneous Inquiries
- **Policy**: Inquiries about terms of service, partnership proposals, or general corporate information not falling under Billing, Technical, Delivery, Account, Product, or Fraud.
- **Resolution**: Provide official FAQ links, standard documentation, or assign to customer relations representative.
- **Classification Category**: Other
- **Priority**: Low
